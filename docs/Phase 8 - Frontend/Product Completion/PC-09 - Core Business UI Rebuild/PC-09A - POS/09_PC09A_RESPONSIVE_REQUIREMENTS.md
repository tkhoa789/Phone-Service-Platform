# PC-09A Responsive Requirements

## Viewports
- **Desktop (1280px+)**: Standard split-pane view (Catalog 2/3 width, Cart 1/3 width).
- **Tablet (768px+)**: Stacked or 50/50 split depending on spatial analysis. Cart must remain highly visible.
- **Mobile (375px+)**: Tabbed interface or strict stacking. A persistent "View Cart (N items)" floating action button or sticky footer may be required to toggle between catalog and cart.

*Critical*: POS is primarily operated on desktop/tablets at the counter. Desktop UX cannot be compromised for mobile compatibility.
